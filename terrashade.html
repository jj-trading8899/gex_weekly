Floor / Ceiling — heaviest put-side support at or below spot, heaviest call-side resistance at or above spot. Position — where spot sits between the two. Bias line — the price where dealer positioning flips; above it hedging dampens moves, below it hedging extends them. Anchor — single largest positioning strike across all expirations.
